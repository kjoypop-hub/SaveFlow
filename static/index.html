How SaveFlow works

  1. Copy the link

    Open the video, then use Share or copy the address from your browser.

  2. Paste and choose

    Paste it above, press Get video, then pick MP4 or MP3 and a quality.

  3. Download

    Press Download. The file is saved by your browser to its usual folder.

Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. SaveFlow works in your browser with no signup and no app.

Which qualities are available?

Only the ones the source video offers. If a video is only 480p, you won't see 1080p.

Why did my link fail?

The video may be private, deleted, or the link may be incomplete. Copy the full link to the exact video and try again.

Can I download private videos?

No. SaveFlow only works with videos that are publicly accessible.
